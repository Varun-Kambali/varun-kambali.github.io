About

I like getting to the root of things. Give me a system that is misbehaving and I'll go looking for the bottleneck that is actually causing it, and for the pattern that isn't obvious at first glance but matters to the people who use it.

I believe in a clear plan, and I also like to explore beyond it: dig a little deeper, sketch the problem out, and land on a solution that fits the problem rather than a generic one. I enjoy leading, and I enjoy a good technical conversation about architecture.

When something has my attention I get fully involved, and done isn't where I stop; the best result I can get is. I love a challenge, within reason, and I make time to enjoy life too.
